# Volunteer Contributors

Part-time and volunteer contributors who apply through the
[contributor interest form](https://forms.gle/RuufBJuckpLuPGhF9) are matched to one or two
projects based on their background. Contributors coordinate with the team on the
[FP Launchpad Zulip](https://fplaunchpad.zulipchat.com).

Interested in contributing? See [fplaunchpad.org/work-with-us](https://fplaunchpad.org/work-with-us/).
